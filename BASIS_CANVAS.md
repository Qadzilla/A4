# P9 — Built, not chosen

> The workspace stops being a catalogue of panels I designed and becomes a
> canvas holding answers Bip built for the questions actually asked.

## What was wrong

Twelve fixed panels. The question only picked which drawer opened. Ask
something unanticipated and you got nothing, or something adjacent. That is
the product deciding what's worth looking at — the exact thing this is meant
to fix for someone who doesn't have a bank's phone number.

Pinning had the same flaw: sorting a list offered as a substitute for
arranging a desk.

## Three changes

**1. Bip builds the answer.** Tools fetch the real figures; Bip composes the
visual for that question and puts it on the canvas. A stock analysis, a tax
breakdown, a comparison of the moves available — whatever the question needs.

The rule that keeps it honest: **it composes the presentation, never the
data.** Only figures a tool returned can appear. "We don't know" stays "we
don't know."

**2. The canvas.** Panels sit where you put them. Drag, pan, zoom, resize, and
write notes beside the numbers. Per year, so it's how you left it.

Not rebuilding A4: no twenty-seven item types, no connection lines, no
nesting. A canvas needs a purpose, and this one has one.

**3. Bip lays out options; it does not recommend.**

Show what is available and what each is worth. Do not rank, do not advise, do
not answer the question behind the question. If someone asks what a sale costs,
say what it costs — do not tell them whether to sell.

## Slices

**S1 — Generated answers.** A tool Bip calls to put a composed visual on the
canvas, rendered sandboxed, styled in the product's own materials. The fixed
panels stay as a fallback and stop being the point.

**S2 — The canvas.** Coordinates, drag, pan, zoom, resize.

**S3 — Notes.** Write anywhere on the canvas.

**S4 — Plain words.** Lead with the meaning, put the term in brackets after.
"Selling within a year of buying is taxed at your normal income rate
(short-term)." Not the reverse.

## The standing rule

Never decide for the user. Not which panel matters, not which move to make,
not what they meant to ask.
