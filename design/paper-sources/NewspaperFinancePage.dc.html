In a Third of Transfers
Delivering Firms Omit Adjusted Basis on Securities Moved Between Houses; Recipients Told They Remain Responsible for the Figure
BY HARLAN W. DEVEREUX  ·  FINANCIAL CORRESPONDENT

BASIS HARBOR, Oct. 13—The Consolidated Basis Association will require member firms to certify adjusted basis on every transferred position beginning with the September settlement. Houses that cannot produce a cost of record must mark the line NOT PROVIDED, leaving the holder to substantiate the figure.

Lott & Farrow reported that of positions received from other houses this season, a third arrived with no cost of record whatever, and a further eighth carried a figure the delivering firm declined to certify.

Where basis cannot be substantiated it is treated as zero, and the entire proceeds are treated as gain. Filers reported a basis of zero on more than eleven thousand transactions last season, most of them on shares transferred between firms.

The association's secretary, Miles A. Corbin, said the certification "imposes no new liability upon the holder, but records plainly where the figure came from, and where it did not come from." Firms will be permitted to furnish an estimate provided it is marked as an estimate.

NO BASIS OF RECORD

Basis is seldom the purchase price. It rises with commissions and with dividends reinvested, and it rises again with any loss disallowed under the wash-sale provision — a loss taken on securities repurchased within thirty days on either side of the sale. The disallowed amount is not forfeited; it is added to the basis of the replacement shares and recovered later.

Reinvested dividends are the commonest omission. A holder who fails to add them reports a smaller basis than he holds and pays tax twice on the same money.

Returns of capital work the other way, reducing basis in the year received, and a holder who has taken them for income over a long term may find the adjusted basis of a position considerably below what he paid for it.

Absent identification at the time of sale, the first shares acquired are treated as the first sold. Lots differ in basis and in holding period, and a position sold one day short of a year is taxed at an entirely different rate.

A specific-lot instruction, given to the broker at or before settlement, is the only means of altering the order, and several houses now decline to accept such instructions by telephone.

ON WHAT BASIS?

"There is no basis for comparison," said a partner at Wash & Wendt. "Two accounts may show the same proceeds and owe nothing alike."

Substitute statements furnished for the current season will carry the legend BASIS NOT REPORTED TO THE SERVICE wherever the delivering firm has not certified the figure, and the box for basis reported will be left unchecked. The obligation to report the correct amount, the association's circular states, remains with the holder in every case.

Where the figure furnished is known to be wrong, the correction is made on the return rather than by the firm: the amount shown is entered as reported, the correction is entered in the adjustment column under code B, and the resulting gain or loss is carried to the summary.

Carryover Trust Company and two other transfer agents were said to be assembling records back to 1962 in order to supply costs on request, a labour one officer described as "archaeology conducted at the client's expense."

NOTICES OF RECORD — BASIS AND ADJUSTMENT
Published pursuant to the rules of the Consolidated Basis Association.

1. Member firms are notified that adjusted basis must be certified upon every transfer of a position between houses, whether the transfer is made for the account of the holder or in settlement between members. Where the delivering firm holds no cost of record, the line shall be marked NOT PROVIDED and the receiving firm shall so advise the holder in writing within ten days of receipt.

2. A figure furnished as an estimate shall be marked as an estimate and shall not be certified. No member shall represent an estimated basis as a cost of record, and no member shall decline to furnish the particulars upon which an estimate was made when they are requested by the holder.

3. Commissions, transfer taxes and fees paid upon acquisition are added to basis. Amounts reinvested in additional shares are added in the year credited. Returns of capital are deducted in the year received. The resulting figure, and not the price first paid, is the adjusted basis of record.

4. Where a loss has been disallowed upon securities repurchased within thirty days before or after a sale, the disallowed amount shall be added to the basis of the replacement shares and shall be carried in the record of that lot until its disposition. The loss is deferred and not forfeited.

5. Absent an instruction identifying the lot, the shares first acquired shall be treated as the shares first sold. Instructions identifying a lot shall be given to the member at or before settlement and shall be recorded in writing; instructions given by telephone will not be accepted after the close of the settlement session.

6. The holding period of a position begins upon the day following the trade date of acquisition and ends upon the trade date of the sale. Positions held one year or less are short-term without regard to the character of the security or the intention of the holder.

7. Substitute statements furnished for the current season shall carry the legend BASIS NOT REPORTED TO THE SERVICE wherever the figure has not been certified, and the box provided for basis reported shall be left unchecked. The obligation to report the correct amount remains in every case with the holder.

8. Where the figure furnished is known to the holder to be incorrect, the correction shall be made upon the return and not by the member: the amount shown is entered as reported, the correction is entered in the adjustment column under code B, and the resulting gain or loss is carried to the summary.

9. Members are reminded that a basis which cannot be substantiated is treated as zero, and that the whole of the proceeds is then treated as gain. The records of acquisition, of reinvestment and of any prior disallowance shall be retained so long as the position is held and for three years thereafter.

10. Transfer agents will furnish costs of record upon written application. Applications relating to acquisitions made before 1962 will be answered as the records permit, and the expense of the search will be borne by the applicant.
